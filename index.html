    About VEGAMAN

    VEGAMAN is a technology company building simple, dependable tools for developers.

    DevKit is version 1 of our free utility collection. It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no backend, no accounts and no tracking. Every tool runs in your browser, and nothing you enter is sent to a server.

    The source is open under the MIT license, so you can read it, change it and host it yourself.
